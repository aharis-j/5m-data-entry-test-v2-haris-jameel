# Question 6 — AI Literacy: Prompt Engineering

**Format:** Short Answer

---

Read both prompts below carefully, then answer the questions that follow.

**Prompt A**

> I am a marketing manager at a retail company and we have just finished a three-month campaign. My team has collected customer feedback through an online survey and we now have about 500 responses stored in a spreadsheet. Each response includes the customer's age group, the product they purchased, their satisfaction rating from 1 to 5, and a short written comment. I need to present the findings to our CEO next Friday in a way that is easy to understand. Can you analyse this data for me, highlight which age groups and products have the lowest satisfaction scores, identify the most common complaints from the written comments, and summarise everything in a short paragraph I can use as an executive summary?

**Prompt B**

> **Role:** You are a data analyst helping a retail marketing team.  
> **Task:** Analyse customer survey data from a 3-month campaign.  
> **Data:** 500 responses containing age group, product purchased, satisfaction rating (1-5), and written comments.  
> **Steps:**
> 1. Identify age groups and products with the lowest satisfaction scores.
> 2. Extract the most common themes from the written comments.
> 3. Summarise findings in an executive summary paragraph.
>
> **Audience:** CEO presentation on Friday.  
> **Constraints:** Keep the summary concise and free of technical jargon.

---

## Task 1 — Analysis

**(a)** Which prompt do you think will get a better response from an AI? State your choice (A or B).

**Answer:**  
B.

---

**(b)** Give **two reasons** to support your choice.

**Reason 1:**  
B is more concise, and gives less room for the AI to go off on a tangent or start doing tasks which are unnecessary. It is dramatically shortened compared to A, yet retains almost all of the necessary information from A.

**Reason 2:**  
B is also more precise/specific in certain areas, which gives less room for ambiguity and subjectivity in the AI's response. For example, B's "... concise and free of technical jargon" is more precise than A's "... in a way that is easy to understand". Another example is A asking for "common complaints" only, versus B asking for "common themes". The AI which uses A might therefore miss out on highlighting the company's areas of strength.

---

**(c)** What is **one strength** of the prompt you did NOT choose?

**Answer:**  
A lays out slightly better context for the task, which the AI might be able to use. For example, A states that the prompter is the marketing manager, but B does not. Stating that the prompter is a manager might be useful to the AI in how it frames information in the report. Also, A states that the survey was done online, but B does not. The AI being told that the survey was online might allow for the AI to consider selection bias relating to specific sub-populations (e.g. respondents without internet access) in caveating the results of the analysis.

---

## Task 2 — Rewrite

Rewrite either prompt by borrowing **one element** from the other to make it stronger. Then explain what you borrowed and why.

**Your rewritten prompt:**

> **Role:** You are a data analyst helping a retail marketing team. The job was requested by the marketing manager.  
> **Task:** Analyse customer survey data from a 3-month campaign, with surveys administered online.  
> **Data:** 500 responses containing age group, product purchased, satisfaction rating (1-5), and written comments.  
> **Steps:**
> 1. Identify age groups and products with the lowest satisfaction scores.
> 2. Extract the most common themes from the written comments.
> 3. Summarise findings in an executive summary paragraph.
>
> **Audience:** CEO presentation on Friday.  
> **Constraints:** Keep the summary concise and free of technical jargon.

**What you borrowed and why:**  
Element borrowed is more context for the prompt.  
1. Added in the "role" section that the job was requested by the marketing manager. This gives the AI more context and can help in how the findings are presented.
2. Added in the "task" section that the survey data was obtained via online surveys from customers
